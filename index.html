FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions clients ask before starting a project with us.

What services does your company provide?

+

We provide software development, web apps development, AI automation, branding and identity, ecommerce solutions, digital marketing, and business consultation.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

+

Yes, we work with startups, small businesses, and growing companies. Our services can be adjusted according to your goals, budget, and business stage.

How long does a project usually take?

+

The timeline depends on the size and type of project. A basic website may take a few weeks, while larger websites or ecommerce platforms may take more time.

Do you help with content for the website?

+

Yes, we can help structure your website content, improve the messaging, and guide you on what text, images, and sections are needed for each page.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

+

Yes, every website we build is designed to work smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens so visitors get a clean experience everywhere.

Can you redesign my existing website?

+

Yes, we can redesign your existing website to make it more modern, mobile friendly, user friendly, and better for conversions.

Do you provide support after project completion?

+

Yes, we can provide ongoing support, maintenance, updates, marketing, and consultation based on your business needs.

Can I choose a custom service package?

+

Yes, we can create a custom package based on your project goals, selected services, timeline, and business requirements.

Can you improve my website speed and SEO?

+

Yes, we can improve website structure, loading speed, on page SEO, technical basics, and content quality to help your site perform better.

How do we start a new project?

+

You can contact us with your project details. We will review your goals, suggest the right approach, and share the next steps, timeline, and package.
