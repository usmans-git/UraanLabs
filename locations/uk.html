CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions by UK Clients

How do we ensure compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act?

We sign binding Data Processing Agreements (DPA) adhering to UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTA). All servers and databases can be pinned exclusively to UK AWS/GCP regions (London) with Zero Data Retention clauses for AI APIs.

How does daily communication and sprint cadence work?

Our senior engineering leads work aligned with London business hours (GMT / BST). We conduct daily asynchronous standups on Slack or Microsoft Teams and hold weekly milestone video calls on Google Meet.

Do you bill in British Pounds (GBP)?

Yes. We provide transparent, fixed-scope milestone invoices denominated in GBP (£) or USD, payable via standard BACS, CHAPS, or international SWIFT wire transfer.

Who owns the intellectual property and code repository?

Your company retains 100% legal ownership of all source code, Figma design assets, schemas, and infrastructure automation scripts from the moment milestones are cleared. Zero recurring licensing or vendor lock-in.

How quickly can we kick off an engineering engagement?

Following an initial 30-minute discovery call, we deliver a formal fixed-price technical scope within 48 hours. Upon agreement, our senior engineering pod kicks off development within 5 business days.
