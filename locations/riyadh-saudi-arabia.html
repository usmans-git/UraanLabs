CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions by Saudi Clients

Are your systems fully compliant with ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing?

Yes. All custom ERP and e-commerce platforms we build for the Saudi market integrate the necessary cryptographic certificate enrollment (CSID), cryptographic hashing, XML signing, and direct API submission to the ZATCA Fatoora clearance platform.

Can you deploy our data inside Saudi Arabia to meet data residency laws?

Yes. We deploy to localized cloud infrastructure including AWS Middle East, Oracle Cloud Dammam, Google Cloud Dammam, or your private on-premise servers in Riyadh to satisfy Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) requirements.

How does communication work during development sprints?

Our senior engineering leads work in Arabia Standard Time (AST / GMT+3). We share daily progress via WhatsApp Business and dedicated Slack channels, with scheduled weekly sprint demos conducted via Microsoft Teams or Google Meet.

Do we own the full source code and intellectual property?

Yes, 100%. All source code, Git repositories, architectural schemas, and design assets are transferred to your company under contract. We build software you own forever with zero recurring per-user license fees.

What payment methods and currencies do you accept?

We invoice on structured, deliverable-backed milestones payable in SAR (Saudi Riyals) or USD via direct international bank wire transfer, providing transparent commercial tax invoices.
