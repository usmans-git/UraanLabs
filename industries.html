SECTOR EXPERTISE

Domain-Specific Engineering Across 7 High-Growth Sectors.

Every industry faces unique regulatory requirements, transaction volumes, and operational friction. We don't apply one-size-fits-all templates—we engineer tailored architectures built for your domain mechanics.

SECTOR 01

Retail & E-Commerce

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: Slow monolithic Shopify/WooCommerce checkout latency, disjointed inventory across multiple warehouses, and manual returns processing.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Headless Next.js storefronts with 400ms page transitions, real-time multi-warehouse inventory synchronization, and automated WhatsApp abandoned cart AI triage.

Compliance & Reliability:

PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway connectors and auto-scaling infrastructure handling Black Friday surges.

SECTOR 02

Education (EdTech)

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: Clunky legacy Moodle portals that discourage student engagement, manual grading burdens on instructors, and zero learner retention telemetry.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Modern custom LMS portals with gamified progression trees, integrated video streaming with zero buffer lag, and 24/7 AI student tutor assistants.

Compliance & Reliability:

COPPA, FERPA, and GDPR student data protection compliance with strict role-based access control.

SECTOR 03

Finance (FinTech & Banking)

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: 48-hour customer KYC friction, audit logging failures, and security vulnerabilities that prevent institutional bank partnerships.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Sub-90-second automated ID & bank statement OCR underwriting, immutable double-entry ledger databases, and high-trust mobile client apps.

Compliance & Reliability:

Continuous VAPT penetration testing, SOC2 Type II audit readiness, and zero-trust VPC architecture.

SECTOR 04

Healthcare

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: Paper-based patient intake, high appointment no-show rates, and insecure transmission of electronic medical records (EMR).

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Encrypted patient mobile portals, automated multi-channel appointment reminder bots, and bi-directional HL7/FHIR EMR integration gateways.

Compliance & Reliability:

HIPAA-compliant cloud storage, end-to-end payload encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

SECTOR 05

Real Estate

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: Slow agent response times causing lost buyer leads, fragmented MLS listing syncs, and manual tenancy document management.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

High-speed interactive property portals with map-based search, instant WhatsApp lead qualification AI bots, and automated lease generation.

Compliance & Reliability:

Real-time RESO Web API synchronization and encrypted digital signature pipelines.

SECTOR 06

Logistics & Supply Chain

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: Bill of Lading (BOL) manual paper data entry, blind spots in fleet shipment tracking, and driver communication delays.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Vision-LLM automated BOL OCR extraction, offline-capable mobile driver companion apps, and real-time GPS telemetry backends.

Compliance & Reliability:

Zero-data-loss dead letter queues and 99.98% backend uptime SLA.

SECTOR 07

Startups & SaaS

Industry Bottlenecks Solved: High developer salaries draining pre-seed runway, endless development cycles that miss launch windows, and messy codebases that fail investor technical due diligence.

Tailored UraanLabs Solutions:

Fixed-scope 4-week production MVP delivery, Stripe customer billing architecture, and clean TypeScript/PostgreSQL codebases ready for Series A auditing.

Compliance & Reliability:

100% intellectual property transfer, automated CI/CD pipelines, and clean architectural documentation.
