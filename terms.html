GOVERNANCE & COMMITMENTS

Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026. Clear, fair, and venture-grade terms governing all engineering, consulting, and design engagements.

Engagement Principles at a Glance

  • Complete IP Assignment: 100% of custom source code, design assets, and architectural documents belong to you upon final invoice settlement.
  • Milestone-Based Invoicing: Clear delivery gates, sprint demos, and code check-ins before milestone funds are disbursed.
  • 30-Day Warranty Period: Bug-fix warranty on all production releases covering functional scope defects.
  • Zero Hidden Lock-In: Fully containerized, cleanly documented repositories deployed to your own cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Azure).

1. Scope of Engagement

Every commercial engagement between UraanLabs ("Studio", "we", "us") and the Client ("Customer", "you") is executed via a Statement of Work (SOW) defining explicit architectural deliverables, timelines, acceptance criteria, and fee structures. In the event of any conflict between these Terms and a specific SOW, the terms of the signed SOW shall take precedence.

2. Intellectual Property (IP) Ownership

Upon full settlement of agreed milestone fees, UraanLabs irrevocably assigns all right, title, and interest in and to all custom deliverables (including source code, UI designs, schemas, and technical documentation) directly to the Client. Any open-source libraries or third-party frameworks incorporated into the build remain governed by their respective permissive licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0).

3. Code Delivery and Hosting Sovereignty

All production builds are deployed directly to Client-owned infrastructure accounts (such as AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Cloudflare, or Vercel). UraanLabs does not hold client software hostage on proprietary proprietary clouds. Clients maintain root administrative access to their code repositories and hosting tenancies at all times.

4. Acceptance Testing & Warranty

Clients are granted a standard 14-day User Acceptance Testing (UAT) window following milestone delivery to verify performance against agreed functional specifications. Following final production handover, UraanLabs provides a complimentary 30-day bug-fix warranty to resolve any defects arising directly from out-of-spec implementation.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Both parties agree to protect and treat all technical, business, and financial data received as confidential. UraanLabs enforces internal data segregation protocols, encrypted laptops, and zero-logging proxy rules to guarantee the security of client assets.

6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms and related agreements shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with applicable commercial laws. In the event of a dispute, both parties commit to an initial 30-day executive consultation in good faith prior to initiating any formal legal or arbitration proceedings.
