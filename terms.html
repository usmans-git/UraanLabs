Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026. Standard commercial conditions governing all software engineering, AI deployment, VAPT auditing, and architecture engagements executed by UraanLabs.

Key Commercial Terms at a Glance

  • Conditional IP Assignment: Custom code and deliverables transfer to the Client strictly upon 100% receipt of cleared milestone funds. UraanLabs withholds all IP rights until full payment.
  • Scope Containment: Features or changes outside the agreed Statement of Work (SOW) require a written Change Order at architectural hourly rates ($75–$150/hr).
  • 7-Day Deemed Acceptance: Sprints and deliverables submitted for review are deemed 100% accepted if no itemized written defects are submitted within 7 calendar days.
  • Liability Cap: UraanLabs' aggregate liability for any engagement is strictly capped at the fees actually received for the specific milestone in dispute.

These Terms of Service ("Terms") constitute a legally binding agreement between UraanLabs ("Studio", "Agency", "we", "us") and the ordering entity ("Client", "Customer", "you"). All projects, architecture discovery calls, and software deployments are executed pursuant to a Statement of Work (SOW) or proposal. In the event of any ambiguity, the signed SOW governs milestone specifics, while these Terms govern all payments, IP retention, liability, and dispute proceedings.

2. Milestone Payments, Deposits & Stop-Work Rights

Engineering sprints and technical allocations are scheduled based on clear financial commitments:

  • Non-Refundable Onboarding Deposits: Project initiation requires an initial deposit (typically 30% to 50% of the SOW valuation). All deposits and sprint disbursements are strictly non-refundable once architectural work or engineering allocations have commenced.
  • Invoicing & Due Dates: Milestone invoices are payable upon presentation (Net-7). Any invoice unpaid after seven (7) calendar days shall accrue a compounding late finance charge of 1.5% per month (or the maximum statutory limit).

3. Intellectual Property Reservation & Background IP

UraanLabs maintains strict protection over its engineering outputs and proprietary tooling:

  • Unauthorized Pre-Payment Use: The Client is granted zero license to deploy, compile, distribute, or commercially exploit any deliverable prior to 100% invoice settlement. Any deployment of unpaid software constitutes willful copyright infringement and an immediate breach of contract.
  • Background IP & Developer Tooling: UraanLabs retains perpetual, global, unencumbered ownership of all pre-existing design tokens, boilerplate repositories, AI agent scaffolding, CI/CD pipelines, cryptographic scripts, and architectural methodologies ("Background IP"). Upon final payment, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such Background IP solely as integrated within the deliverable.

4. Scope Creep, Change Orders & 7-Day Deemed Acceptance

To ensure project momentum and protect against uncompensated scope expansion:

  • Strict Scope Boundaries: UraanLabs is obligated to build only the deliverables explicitly itemized in the approved SOW. Any extra feature, altered user flow, third-party API substitution, or design overhaul requested by the Client constitutes an out-of-scope "Change Order", billable at UraanLabs' standard architectural rate of $75 to $150 per hour or a separate fixed addendum.
  • 7-Day Deemed Acceptance: Following milestone delivery to staging or git commit check-in, the Client is granted seven (7) calendar days to perform verification. If the Client does not submit an itemized written defect notice within this 7-day period, the milestone shall be deemed irrevocably approved, accepted, and cleared for milestone disbursement.
