PRODUCTION BLUEPRINTS

Real-World Architectural Deployments

Practical use-case breakdowns demonstrating how we engineer solutions around operational bottlenecks.

BLUEPRINT 01 // EDTECH PLATFORM

Real-Time Assessment Portal Supporting 25,000 Concurrent Students

The Business Bottleneck:

An online education provider was experiencing regular server crashes during nationwide exam windows due to database lock contention.

Business Impact: Achieved 99.99% uptime during peak exam windows; database response times dropped from 2.8s to 45ms with zero lost submissions.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Decomposed the monolithic exam engine into stateless microservices backed by Redis queues.
  2. Implemented optimistic client-side answer caching with background sync to handle intermittent drops.
  3. Configured PostgreSQL read replicas with AWS Aurora Serverless auto-scaling.
  4. Simulated 50,000 concurrent student connections via distributed load testing prior to release.
BLUEPRINT 02 // LOGISTICS PORTAL

B2B Freight Brokerage & Dispatch Management Portal

The Business Bottleneck:

A freight brokerage managed dispatch operations across 12 spreadsheets, resulting in double-booked trucks and billing disputes.

Business Impact: Automated 1,200 daily load assignments; dispatch cycle time dropped by 72% within the first 60 days of launch.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Engineered a custom React dashboard featuring live map integration and driver telemetry.
  2. Built automated carrier rate matching algorithms calculating real-time margin optimization.
  3. Integrated Twilio SMS and WhatsApp notifications dispatching load details to drivers.
  4. Connected automated invoicing webhooks directly into QuickBooks Enterprise.
