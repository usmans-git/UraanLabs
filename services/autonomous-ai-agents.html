BLUEPRINT 01 // INVESTMENT DUE DILIGENCE

Autonomous Competitor & Market Intelligence Research Agent

The Business Bottleneck:

A private equity firm spent 40 associate hours weekly compiling competitive landscape memos and financial filing benchmarks for deal reviews.

Business Impact: Research memo generation accelerated from 5 days to 22 minutes; comprehensive market data coverage increased by 300%.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Engineered a Coordinator Agent that decomposes a target sector into 6 specific research sub-tasks.
  2. Specialized Web Scraper Agent queries SEC EDGAR filings, news feeds, and patent registries.
  3. Financial Analyst Agent normalizes EBITDA margins and revenue multiples into structured tables.
  4. Reviewer Agent verifies factual citations and compiles a formatted 15-page PDF executive memo.
BLUEPRINT 02 // B2B SALES PROSPECTING

Autonomous Lead Research & Personalized Outreach Agent

The Business Bottleneck:

A cybersecurity SaaS sales team spent 65% of their day manually researching prospect tech stacks and writing individual cold emails.

Business Impact: Outreach volume scaled 4x with zero rep expansion; cold email reply rate climbed from 2.1% to 8.4% due to hyper-specific technical personalization.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Agent queries DNS records, job board postings, and LinkedIn profiles to map prospect tech stacks.
  2. Identifies exact CVE vulnerabilities associated with the prospect’s exposed cloud infrastructure.
  3. Drafts custom, non-spammy technical email proposals referencing specific architecture findings.
  4. Queues drafts in human-in-the-loop sales review queue before dispatching through Outreach/SalesLoft.
