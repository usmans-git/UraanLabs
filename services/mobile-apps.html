BLUEPRINT 01 // DIGITAL HEALTH

HIPAA-Compliant Remote Patient Monitoring & Telehealth Mobile App

The Business Bottleneck:

A healthcare startup needed an iOS and Android app connecting Bluetooth medical sensors (pulse oximeters, blood pressure monitors) to patient charts.

Business Impact: Over 45,000 active patients onboarded; app maintains a 4.9-star rating across 3,200 App Store reviews with zero data leaks.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Engineered native BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) background synchronization drivers in Flutter.
  2. Encrypted all patient health metrics on-device using AES-256 SQLCipher before cloud transmission.
  3. Integrated WebRTC for encrypted sub-second peer-to-peer video consultations with doctors.
  4. Successfully cleared Apple HealthKit and medical privacy compliance audits.
BLUEPRINT 02 // ON-DEMAND LOGISTICS

Driver Dispatch & Navigation App with Real-Time Telemetry

The Business Bottleneck:

A last-mile delivery fleet experienced frequent app crashes and GPS battery drain among 800 drivers using low-cost Android smartphones.

Business Impact: Driver battery life extended by 40%; daily successful delivery completion jumped from 89% to 98.4%.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Implemented adaptive GPS throttling algorithms updating server coordinates only on significant motion.
  2. Built offline route caching allowing drivers to complete deliveries inside underground parking structures.
  3. Integrated digital signature capture and barcode scanning with native camera acceleration.
  4. Automated CI/CD release builds via Fastlane deploying directly to Google Play internal tracks.
