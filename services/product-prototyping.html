BLUEPRINT 01 // FINTECH SEED STAGE

Clickable WealthTech Prototype Securing $2.5M Seed Round

The Business Bottleneck:

Two non-technical founders needed to demonstrate an algorithmic portfolio rebalancing engine to Silicon Valley angel investors without writing code.

Business Impact: Closed $2.5M seed funding within 4 weeks of demo deployment; prototype served as the blueprint for subsequent engineering sprints.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Mapped the end-to-end user onboarding, bank linking (simulated Plaid flow), and risk allocation journey.
  2. Engineered realistic interactive charting using Figma Smart Animate and Framer components.
  3. Recorded a polished 90-second product demo for inclusion in their DocSend pitch deck.
  4. Tested the prototype with 8 retail investors to refine the portfolio rebalancing user experience.
BLUEPRINT 02 // ENTERPRISE HRTECH

Validating Global Payroll Compliance Tool Before Writing Code

The Business Bottleneck:

An HR consultancy wanted to launch a SaaS tool for international contractor compliance, but was uncertain which payroll integrations were mandatory.

Business Impact: User testing revealed that automated contractor tax deduction was 3x more valued than timesheet tracking, saving 4 months of engineering effort.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Built an interactive dual-persona prototype (Admin vs Contractor view) in 10 business days.
  2. Conducted moderated usability sessions with 6 HR directors across US and UK companies.
  3. Refactored the core information architecture based on empirical user session feedback.
  4. Handed off complete engineering specs directly to the development team.
