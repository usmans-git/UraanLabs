PRODUCTION BLUEPRINTS

Real-World Architecture Deployments

How we solve complex business bottlenecks without reliance on hype or hand-waving.

BLUEPRINT 01 // RETAIL & LOGISTICS

Multi-Vendor Invoice & PO Reconciliation Engine

The Business Bottleneck:

An e-commerce distributor receives 1,400 monthly invoices via email. A 4-person accounting team spends 25 hours weekly cross-checking line items against ERP inventory records, causing 5-day payment delays and recurring ledger errors.

Business Impact: Processing time cut from 5 days to 4 minutes; manual overhead reduced by 84% with zero ledger drift.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Ingestion: Email webhooks deposit raw PDF invoices into an encrypted AWS S3 bucket.
  2. Extraction: A containerized Vision-LLM parser converts PDFs into strict JSON Schema payloads.
  3. Deterministic Validation: Microservice executes database checks against SAP/PostgreSQL ERP purchase orders.
  4. Auto-Settlement: Balanced invoices automatically post to QuickBooks/Xero APIs; discrepancies route to Slack diff queues.
BLUEPRINT 02 // FINTECH & BANKING

Real-Time Digital KYC & Document Underwriting Pipeline

The Business Bottleneck:

A digital credit provider faces a 42% sign-up drop-off because manual verification of national ID cards and bank statements takes 48 to 72 hours per borrower.

Business Impact: Verification completed in under 90 seconds; user onboarding completion improved by 31%.
The Engineered Architecture:
  1. Secure Ingestion: Applicant uploads identity documents via signed temporary URLs.
  2. PII Scrubbing: Local container parses statements, immediately hashing sensitive tax numbers.
  3. Agentic Risk Scoring: Fine-tuned model analyzes statement cash-flow patterns and income stability.
  4. Instant Triage: Verified records trigger backend webhook account approvals with automated audit trails.
