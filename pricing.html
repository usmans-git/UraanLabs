Pricing FAQs

Questions About Pricing

Here are quick answers to common questions about packages, payments, revisions, and custom quotes.

Can I customize a pricing package?

+

Yes, every package can be adjusted based on your required pages, features, timeline, integrations, and support needs.

Do you charge monthly or per project?

+

Most website and branding projects are charged per project. Monthly support, SEO, marketing, and maintenance can be added separately.

Do I need to pay everything upfront?

+

For most projects, we start with an initial deposit and collect the remaining payment based on agreed milestones or before final delivery.

Can I upgrade my package later?

+

Yes, you can upgrade as your business grows. We can add more pages, features, marketing support, SEO, or ongoing maintenance when needed.

Are revisions included in the package?

+

Yes, revisions are included according to the selected plan. Extra revisions can be added if more changes are needed.

How do I choose the right plan?

+

If you need a simple business website, Starter is enough. If you need stronger design, SEO structure, and lead capture, Professional is better. For custom systems, choose Enterprise.

Are hosting and domain included?

+

Hosting and domain costs are usually separate, but we can guide you through setup or include them in a custom package if required.

Do you offer monthly support plans?

+

Yes, monthly plans are available for website updates, maintenance, SEO, digital marketing, content support, and performance improvements.
