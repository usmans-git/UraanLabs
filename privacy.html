TRANSPARENCY & TRUST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. At UraanLabs, privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental architectural requirements, not an afterthought.

Executive Privacy Summary

  • Zero AI Training: We never use client source code, confidential business data, or proprietary datasets to train public or shared foundation models.
  • Client Ownership: You retain 100% intellectual property rights and full data ownership of all deliverables upon project sign-off.
  • Encryption Standards: All data in transit is protected via TLS 1.3; data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 with managed KMS keys.
  • Strict NDAs: Every engagement is governed by mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements before architecture discovery begins.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit an inquiry form, schedule an architecture consultation, or communicate with our engineering team via email or WhatsApp. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Name, work email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, and geographical region.
  • Project Scope Information: Technical requirements, estimated budget ranges, timeline targets, and architecture briefs.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized server logs, browser user agents, and IP addresses for DDoS mitigation, performance monitoring, and security auditing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process client information strictly for legitimate commercial and engineering purposes:

  • To formulate tailored technical proposals, architectural blueprints, and resource estimates.
  • To perform contractually agreed-upon software engineering, AI deployment, VAPT auditing, or brand design services.
  • To comply with international tax, corporate governance, and regulatory requirements.

3. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Under our master service agreements, all client source code, proprietary algorithms, databases, API credentials, and trade secrets remain the sole property of the client. UraanLabs acts solely as a technical implementation partner. Our engineers work within isolated sandbox environments and enforce principle-of-least-privilege access controls.

4. Third-Party Services and Sub-Processors

We partner only with industry-standard, SOC2-certified cloud infrastructure providers (such as AWS, Cloudflare, GitHub, and Postmark) to deliver hosting, DNS, code versioning, and transactional communications. We never sell, rent, or monetize your contact or business data to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Alignment)

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain the right to request access to any personal data we hold, request corrections, or request immediate permanent deletion of your inquiry and contact records. To exercise these rights, email us at info@uraanlabs.com.
